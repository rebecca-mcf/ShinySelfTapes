About Rebecca

Rebecca was born just off-Broadway in New York City and has been performing ever since.

She holds a degree in Performing and Screen Arts (Acting) and has worked professionally in the screen industry for over 17 years in both Los Angeles and Auckland.

Her credits include a recurring role on Shortland Street and lead roles in feature films, television series, short films, and commercials.

Rebecca loves helping actors build confidence on camera and deliver authentic performances.
